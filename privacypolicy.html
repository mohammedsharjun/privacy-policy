SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS

  • What personal information do we process? We process basic account identifiers (name, email) for registration, device identifiers for app diagnostics, and approximate/precise location exclusively on-device for Islamic prayer times and Qibla compass direction.
  • Do we process sensitive personal information? We do not process sensitive personal information such as biometric ID data, genetic data, or sexual orientation. Religious learning preferences are chosen voluntarily by you to personalize your curriculum.
  • In-App Purchases: Virtual purchases (Gems/Diamonds) are processed through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card or banking details.
  • Microphone / Speech: Microphone access is used strictly on-device for real-time speech recitation practice. We do not store or transmit raw audio recordings.
  • Advertising: We display non-personalized or personalized ads via Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play Developer policies.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal Information Disclosed by You

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services or contact customer support:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, and hashed authentication password (secured via Google Firebase Authentication).
  • Learning Progress: Quiz scores, streak days, lessons completed, and virtual rewards.
  • Support Inquiries: Messages, feedback, and correspondence sent to our support email.

Information Collected Automatically & Device Permissions

  • Geolocation Data (Prayer Times & Qibla): We request permission to access your device's approximate and precise location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION and ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION). This data is processed strictly locally on your device to calculate accurate astronomical prayer times and determine the Qibla compass bearing. Your location is never stored on our external servers or tracked in the background.
  • Microphone / Audio (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only during active recitation practice to analyze speech pronunciation in real-time. No audio clips are saved or transmitted to external storage.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to trigger punctual prayer time reminders (Athan) and study streak notifications at user-specified moments.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: Device model, operating system version, crash reports (via Google Firebase Crashlytics), and app performance metrics.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

We process your personal information for specific legitimate business purposes:

  • To facilitate secure account registration and authentication.
  • To calculate accurate prayer schedules and Qibla direction based on astronomical coordinates.
  • To save and synchronize your educational curriculum progress across devices.
  • To fulfill in-app virtual purchases via Google Play Billing.
  • To send scheduled daily learning reminders and Athan alerts.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and enhance application stability.

3. WHAT LEGAL BASES DO WE RELY ON?

We process your personal information under the following legal frameworks (e.g., GDPR, UK GDPR, and applicable local privacy laws):

  • Consent: When you grant explicit runtime permissions (Location, Microphone, Notifications). You can revoke permissions at any time in device settings.
  • Contract Performance: To deliver the core services, manage user profiles, and record learning milestones.
  • Legitimate Interests: To protect our platform from fraud, ensure data security, and maintain software reliability.

4. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR DATA?

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with certified infrastructure providers necessary to operate the application:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud Firestore database, user authentication, and crash logging.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): In-app ad monetization. AdMob may collect device advertising IDs (GAID) in accordance with Google Play policies.
  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC): In-app purchase processing.
  • Cloudinary: Secure CDN used to stream educational illustrations and audio files.

5. DATA RETENTION AND ACCOUNT DELETION

We retain your personal information only as long as your account remains active with us.

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated personal data instantly at any time by going to: Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • Manual Request: You can also email us at deeniac.team@gmail.com to request full data purge. Upon request, all account records and progress are permanently purged within 30 days.

6. SECURITY MEASURES

We have implemented rigorous technical and organizational security measures:

  • All data transmissions between the application and servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Authentication tokens are securely stored and validated through Firebase Authentication.
  • Financial transactions are handled entirely by Google Play's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.
